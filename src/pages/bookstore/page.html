The documentation application

From the delivery box to the shop window.

Manage a catalog, receive stock, and publish product pages using the same data. Chapter House starts with ordinary HTML and forms, then grows one useful workflow at a time.

Open your backoffice →Browse the example shop

1. Prepare a product

Create a book with a SKU, URL, description, and price. New products start as drafts with no stock.

2. Receive a delivery

Record incoming copies with a delivery reference. Stock and its receipt are committed together.

3. Publish your shop

Publish the product to your personal demonstration storefront. Other signed-in users have separate catalogs.

This first working chapter covers catalog, receiving, and publication. Sales, reservations, customer management, and shipping will build on this model; checkout is not implemented yet.
